## Overview

Loader is the Pada 1.x loading indicator. Since 2.0, use **Spinner** for inline loading,
**ProgressCircle** when progress is known, or **Skeleton** for content placeholders. It keeps working
until 3.0.

## Use with AI

```text
Replace Pada Loader usages: use Spinner for inline waits, ProgressCircle where a percentage is
available, and Skeleton for initial page loads.
```
