# Motion

Quick, settled motion built on duration and easing tokens. Durations are multiplied by
`--pada-motion-scale`, which is 0 under `prefers-reduced-motion` or `data-motion="reduced"`.
Enter motion uses `@starting-style`; exits wait for their transition before the element leaves.

<MotionScale />

- `standard` for most changes, `enter` and `exit` for things appearing and leaving, `emphasized`
  for large surfaces, `spring` for small playful moments.
- Animate `opacity` and `transform`; avoid animating layout.
