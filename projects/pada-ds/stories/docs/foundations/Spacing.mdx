# Spacing & density

Space is `n × 0.25rem × --pada-density`. `data-density` sets the density to 0.85 (compact), 1
(comfortable) or 1.15 (spacious), so one attribute tightens or loosens every layout. Try the
**Density** toolbar.

## Control heights

Buttons, inputs and selects share three heights, which follow density too.
